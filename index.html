Core Capabilities

Built for Reliability, Control, and Simplicity

Designed for dual-phone users, on-call engineers, and privacy-conscious professionals who need critical messages forwarded reliably.

WHEN-IF-THEN Builder

Compose rules visually: monitor incoming cellular messages and forward them based on specific sender phone numbers, alphanumeric company IDs (e.g., BANK, ALERTS), or content keywords.

Inbound Text MMS

Seamlessly handles the text bodies of incoming cellular MMS messages via least-privilege system telephony providers, without ever scanning your personal historical inbox.

Dual-SIM Route Selection

Equipped for dual-SIM phones: specify which cellular subscription (Default, SIM 1, or SIM 2) transmits forwarded dispatches with crystal-clear carrier labels (e.g. SIM 1 — Orange).

Truthful Transport Status

No false delivery assumptions: distinguishes between Submitted to Android telephony, Sent by the cellular radio chip, and Delivered via mobile carrier delivery receipts.

Local JSON Backup & Restore

Export and import automation rules as schema-validated JSON files via Android's Storage Access Framework. Backups contain only automation rules—never SMS text or personal data.

Loop & Duplicate Protection

In-memory SHA-256 fingerprinting suppresses rapid carrier broadcast echoes and prevents infinite ping-pong forwarding loops between linked automated devices.

Security by Design

Zero Internet Permission. 100% On-Device.

Most SMS utilities upload your confidential messages and 2FA authentication codes to cloud servers. SMS Automations does not request the Android Internet permission, keeping message processing strictly local on your device.

Zero Internet Access

The android.permission.INTERNET permission is completely absent from the Android Manifest. Under the Android security model, the OS prevents the app from opening network sockets or uploading data to remote servers.

No Stored Message Bodies

Text contents and sensitive OTP / banking codes exist exclusively in volatile device RAM during rule evaluation and are discarded immediately. Nothing is written to disk.

Masked Activity History

In-app diagnostics and execution histories retain a maximum of 100 entries with masked phone numbers (e.g. +336****78). Zero raw message bodies are displayed.

No Cloud Telemetry

Contains zero third-party advertising frameworks, zero behavioral analytics SDKs, zero crash tracking beacons, and requires no user accounts.

Transparent Technical Limitations

We believe in complete transparency. Please review these technical boundaries prior to installing:

  • 1. Standard Cellular SMS / MMS Only SMS Automations processes standard cellular SMS and text MMS. It does not support RCS ("Chat features" in Google Messages), Apple iMessage, WhatsApp, or Signal. Senders must send standard cellular messages.
  • 2. MMS Media Attachments Omitted For inbound multimedia messages (MMS), only the plain-text body is evaluated and forwarded. Photos, videos, and audio attachments are omitted in V1.
  • 3. Mobile Carrier SMS Billing & Unencrypted Transport Forwarded messages are transmitted through your mobile carrier as standard cellular SMS, and your carrier's standard messaging rates apply. Please note: standard cellular SMS is transmitted over telecommunications networks and is not end-to-end encrypted. While SMS Automations executes all routing rules strictly locally on your physical device, message contents sent over cellular radio are subject to standard mobile carrier telecommunications transport.
