What it does

A clear path from message to action.

Build forwarding rules on your phone, then let the app handle supported incoming cellular messages locally.

Set your forwarding rules

Choose the conditions that decide when a supported incoming message should be forwarded.

Forward to cellular numbers

Route supported SMS and text content from MMS to a phone number through cellular messaging.

Keep processing on device

Rules run locally on Android. The app requests no Internet permission and does not persist message bodies to disk.

Privacy by design

Your message content stays on your device.

SMS Automations processes supported incoming messages locally. The Android app has no Internet permission, includes no analytics SDK, and does not save message bodies to persistent storage.

Read the full Privacy Policy
